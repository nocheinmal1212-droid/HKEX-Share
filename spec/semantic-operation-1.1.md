# Direct-contributor headers, operation 1.1.0

This is the active bounded header operation. The [1.0 contract](semantic-operation.md), original
requests, answers, annotations, reviews and evaluations remain historical and unchanged. The
four-field response `SCHEMA` value is unchanged. No numerical comparison is authorized by a header
annotation. Unknown currency, scale, precision and accounting completeness remain planning
prerequisites; no arithmetic, OCR or financial-context resolver is added here.

## Source prerequisites and support

`semantic.project` validates the selected evidence IR and preserves the complete selected table,
literal labels, fragments and spans. The amount-mask/amount-varied transformations remain unchanged.
`header_support.analyze_source` derives host metadata from the source, before any answer. It binds
the byte hash of [the finite vocabulary](../src/hkex_audit/header_support_policy.json) and its analysis
version. Evaluator support arrays and fixture IDs are never runtime inputs. The model receives
only the evidence context, prompt and unchanged response schema, not the host's derived support list.

A potential context group is a nonblank text cell spanning multiple columns with a wholly contained
lower header occurrence. Lower means starting at or below the group's bottom row. The header band
ends at the target's bottom row. Full column intervals establish containment; same-row siblings are
not ancestors. Intervals must be laminar and the immediate child partition must cover the group
exactly. Unknown groups, unsupported content, incomplete or crossing partitions block the source.

Literal category, period and measurement labels qualify only in combination with that partition,
a descendant total marker, at least two non-total branches, and qualified nested groups. This
recognizes contextual membership, not accounting addends. Contradictory literal period/dimension
paths block even if a wrapper cannot qualify. A candidate with a discriminator absent at the target
also blocks; such additional diagnostics do not certify the unqualified wrapper. Different nested
category keys block unless a future reviewed policy defines their relationship.

For an eligible answer, require the target, every contributor, and **every qualified group ancestor
of either** in `support_refs`. A selected subtotal can require its revenue group even when the outer
target is outside that group. Contributors must remain inside each qualified target group. Known
disjoint groups do not become required support. Whole-label continuation wrappers are presentation
context; a recognized full-width common-unit wrapper is also presentation context. Single-column
common units outside contributor groups are retained but optional. A unit label over only one
competing spanning branch is not assumed common. Unknown contexts block rather than invent a role.

## Separate subtotal relationship

Containment never establishes that a subtotal summarizes its siblings. The sole initial summary
rule recognizes the contract-revenue group with exactly three immediate literal branches: one
unqualified total, one recognition-at-a-point label and one recognition-over-time label. Roles must
be unique and the partition complete, with no extras, blank branches or conflicting context. The
versioned policy supplies literal vocabulary; source relationships supply occurrence IDs. Order and
amounts do not choose the endpoints. Independent source review supports this finite semantic premise;
it is not a generic rule that all subtotal peers are addends.

Persist a separate `summary_scopes` witness with rule, group, subtotal, component and support IDs.
Every possible non-target total contributor remaining after known target-branch exclusions needs
an established scope; otherwise block with `unresolved_subtotal_scope`. The target itself is exempt
from that contributor-scope test. Reject simultaneous selection of a subtotal and any component
reachable through established summary edges; cycles fail. A blocked source retains reasons but no
certified summary witnesses. A period wrapper never inherits a summary relation from containment.
An outer answer need not cite unselected timing components merely because the witness records them.

## Associated prose and states

Classify table-associated non-cell subtrees from **validated IR before projection**. Only a single
whole literal caption matching the finite caption pattern, or a whole continuation marker, is
permitted. Multiple literal occurrences in one subtree block as `unestablished_text_order` even
with distinct ordinals. `ordinal` is occurrence identity metadata, not a declared reading-order
capability, and is not added to the projection. A whole caption with null ordinal is allowed.
Separate notes, appended restrictions, other prose and nonliteral context block. Whole-string
matching cannot consume a caption prefix and silently discard its restriction.

`invalid_extraction` and genuinely blank/missing-target `missing_evidence` precede `blocked_scope`.
The latter is a **local source prerequisite outcome**, not a model response state. It makes zero
calls and produces no model annotation, including no empty abstention annotation. New G3, G4
revision 2 and G5 operational expectations are blocked; their independently reviewed semantic
states remain respectively missing evidence, ambiguity and incompatible context. Those semantic
judgments are not claimed to be enforced by citation membership.

On eligible inputs, semantic abstentions retain the four-field shape with empty contributors and
target/context citations. Wrong IDs, missing citations, incompatible branches, overlapping summary
selection, contradictory abstention shape and edited projections fail closed. The reason for a
schema-valid abstention and the completeness/correctness of an otherwise valid contributor set
still require independent semantic evaluation. Arbitrary natural-language entailment, inline-cell
notes, external notes and extractor association fidelity are not certified by this gate.

## Binding and acceptance

Queries bind operation/prompt/schema, selected source hash, source selection, gate and recomputable
host analysis. Consumption reprojects from selected IR; a caller cannot edit context, rehash it and
thereby bypass source binding. Attempt/provenance and replay details are in the [1.1 attempt
contract](paired-semantic-attempts-1.1.md). Runtime metadata is interpretation, not extracted text.

All existing and authored fixtures are exposed development evidence. Source-only agent review is
not human/domain approval. The historical **0/4 complete frozen support** result is unchanged.
No new model behavior, saved-IR semantic acceptance or Milestone 2 completion is established.
